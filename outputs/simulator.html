How to read this. A logistic model of whether each approved scheme lapsed, fitted on Planning London Datahub records with Bank of England, ONS and UK HPI series. It captures average patterns, not the fate of a single site: out-of-sample it ranks schemes only moderately well (AUC ), so read changes, not exact levels. Ranges come from refitting the model on 200 resamples of whole boroughs.

What it cannot see. Land prices, developer finance terms and viability assessments are not public. Rates and costs rose together in 2022–23, so their separate effects are estimated with uncertainty. These are associations, not proof of cause. "Starts" means recorded commencement, which can be a token start.
